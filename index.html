2026

      

        
Agentic Football Cup — Berlin Agents of Football

        
Team entry in AWS's agentic football league: five autonomous agents built on the
          Strands Agents SDK (Amazon Bedrock AgentCore) playing 5v5 football in real time —
          2-second decision ticks, a 1-second latency budget, and free-text touchline coaching
          mid-match. The Alpha Season drew 12,000+ registered teams worldwide. Team name and
          placement deliberately kept off the record.

2026-10

      

        
Siemens Mobility Hackathon — Supplier Risk Intelligence

        
Weekend sprint for procurement teams: a Next.js dashboard over 10,000 anonymized supplier
          risk profiles, plus a Python deep-research agent that investigates real companies
          on the public web, scores eight risk-dimension groups in parallel and appends them to the
          dataset. Martin authored the agent: parallel sub-agents per risk criterion, tiered model
          routing, live SSE progress — ~30 commits in a single day. DuckDB under the hood,
          Nebius Token Factory LLM, Tavily web search.
